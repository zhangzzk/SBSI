## Current evidence: who passes versus who crosses

At zero shear the selected-neighbour diagnostic is E[S B]/E[S], where S is the joint measured/generative cut and B a fixed reference scene coefficient. Even an exact primary-shear derivative of each conditional mean radius/flux does not establish the correct joint distribution at zero shear. Nor does it determine a change in membership at finite shear: the galaxies and ellipticities moving across the two boundaries matter. For the selected mean shape, locally d⟨e⟩sel/dg = (dE[S e]/dg − ⟨e⟩sel dE[S]/dg)/E[S]; evaluate the actual ratios on each finite leg. The corresponding selected-neighbour numerator is E[S B]. B is a proxy, not the true neighbour shear response.

The current code only changes disk shape transport when neighbour shear flips at fixed primary shear. It reuses the same generated size, flux and selection. A loss based on primary shear cannot give this model a nonzero neighbour-only photometric response. [Four-corner code](../primary_neighbour_replay_20261107/predict.py). This issue is separate from photometric sensitivity to *static* neighbour configuration, which the frozen-shape run demonstrably learned better. With fixed shape weights, adding the existing neighbour loss reduced the held-out unsheared selected-score error from +0.003367 to +0.000762 (paired change −0.002605 ±0.000027, whole-case jackknife); constant-shear evaluation was still in progress at this review. [Own-data result](../frozen_shape_neighbour_20261107/own_results.md).

**Diagnostic from existing images only (Slurm 17815772):** retain the 1,012 of 2,048 fixed probability-sampled IDs detected with finite photometry and positive radius in all four corners (254/255/252/251 per field). Ignore fitted ngmix shapes and hold each primary's catalogue intrinsic e1 fixed. Apply each of the nine cuts independently to the four saved photometries. At radius >3 px/mag <25, flipping only **neighbour** shear from −0.02 to +0.02 gives **10 entries, 8 exits, 13 distinct changers** across the two fixed-primary signs (2,024 comparisons, not independent trials). Thus isolated neighbour shear can change measured cut membership. All three stored endpoints have identical generated pass probabilities under this flip. The projected neighbour selection response of the fixed intrinsic ellipticity is +0.03264 with four-case jackknife SE 0.01835 and conditional stratified-primary SE 0.02382. Do not add those errors: the sign/amplitude is not well resolved. This is neither measured-shape R11 nor m; common detection omits detection migration. For the primary flip, the corresponding measured projected response is +0.02307 (conditional SE 0.02725); the nine-cut endpoint gives +0.04687, paired measured-minus-model −0.02379 (conditional SE 0.02863). This small pilot does not establish a primary selection-response failure of a particular size. [All cuts, errors and receipts](boundary.json), [reducer](boundary.py). Simulation-minus-model signs here follow [conventions](../../CONVENTIONS.md#6-response-estimators).

## What would discriminate

First compare the GalSBI flow and **its own held-out paired data** on primary shear: orientation-resolved changes of measured magnitude and log-radius near each boundary, the passing fraction, and the selected e and B numerators on both legs. Resolve differences by neighbour strength. Start with the untouched nine-cut and both frozen-shape endpoints; do not tune from the four development images. A wrong slope *and* wrong shape-weighted migration would support a targeted photometry-only auxiliary loss on those training observables, checked across the nine cuts and against the joint likelihood. Matching a smooth-cut training target must be confirmed with hard cuts. If the remaining problem is neighbour-only migration, require an input or photometry/usable-event transport that actually responds to neighbour shear plus separately sheared training labels. Do not infer a need for that extension from the positive point estimate of this small pilot. No training, new images or checkpoint selection were performed in this retrospective.
