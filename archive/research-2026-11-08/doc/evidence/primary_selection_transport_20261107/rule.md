## Estimands and uncertainty

For each galaxy let h=0.05, n=g/h and u=intrinsic zero-leg ellipticity projected onto n; v is each leg's own fitted/generated shape projected onto n. Positive residual means measured minus model. At each of nine independent hard cuts compare leg pass fractions, selected B, selected u and selected v, and both unnormalized shape and B numerators. Directional response is a finite difference of the two separately normalized means divided by h. Fixed-u response isolates membership; v includes both shape and membership. These random-direction single-primary statistics are neither individual coherent R11/R22 nor multiplicative bias. Also report raw and orientation-regressed photometric changes: slope of (q1−q0)/h on u with a free intercept, for q=MAG_AUTO, ln(FLUX_RADIUS), and bounded local observables clip(m−m_cut,±0.15 mag) and clip(ln(r/r_cut),±0.08). Bounded observables integrate motion across a band, without conditioning a model draw on the measured zero-leg photometry. No clipping in global observables.
