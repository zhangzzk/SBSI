# Primary versus neighbour shear: operational pilot

## Completed pilot: where does the change in response go?

All 4,096 native mixed exposures, all eight coherent controls and all four model replays finished; maximum discrepancy against the previous full-parent model response was 2.00e-15. At the central cut (output radius > 3 pixels, magnitude < 25), the simulation gives primary R11 = 0.76969, neighbour R11 = 0.26425, and coherent R11 = 1.03394. The first two sum to the third by construction. These contrasts change the shear applied to the primary light or to all other light, respectively. They are not identical to the flow and emulator terms in the model.

**Paired changes are much more precise than the absolute residuals.** Subtracting residuals for two frozen models on identical galaxies cancels the measured shape from the comparison. Positive values below mean the new model predicts *less* response. Errors are paired delete-one-scene jackknife SEs on four development fields. The finite-frame sampling SEs for the first row are 0.00238 / 0.00015 / 0.00237. The two uncertainty estimates must not be added.

At the central cut every scene individually shows a larger shift under primary shear than under neighbour shear for likelihood → nine-cut, for all three flag/selection choices. Across nine cuts **pooled over the scenes**, the primary accounts for 91–95% (predicted/generated), 93–97% (actual/generated), and 93–100% (actual/measured) of the coherent shift. Individual-scene ratios are unstable when the coherent difference is small. The physical primary-shear contrast corroborates the earlier [conditional-shape factor result](../neighbour_factor_swap_20261107/findings.md#final-conclusion-both-shear-axes); they measure different things. The added neighbour moment shifts the predicted neighbour response upward, but measured selection attenuates the change. The auxiliary *selected-neighbour coefficient* and the response to physically shearing neighbours are distinct.

**Absolute attribution remains unresolved.** With predicted usability/generated selection, nine-cut simulation-minus-model residuals are primary +0.05915 ± 0.07540, neighbour +0.09042 ± 0.09266, coherent +0.14956 ± 0.07256 (case jackknife). Conditional stratified sampling SEs are 0.08127 / 0.08860 / 0.12851. Four-case scatter alone understates the uncertainty relevant to subsampling the original images. The earlier full-catalogue coherent simulation R11 on these same fields was 0.90581; its nine-cut predicted-usability residual was +0.00736 ± 0.00641 (case jackknife). The small pilot uses different fit realizations and only about 500 selected primaries per leg. Its +0.14956 is not a revised full-catalogue bias. The [absolute results table](results.md) has all three endpoints and flag/selection modes; [report.json](report.json) has all nine cuts, R11/R21, case and sampling covariance, optimizer, and precision projections. g2 was not injected, so this pilot cannot measure R22.

**What the two fit repeats change.** On exactly the same images with fixed detections, centroids and photometry, the nine-cut predicted/generated primary residual is −0.06301 or +0.18130, and the neighbour residual is +0.18813 or −0.00729. The selected sets agree exactly under both fits: 507 / 505 / 501 / 502 selected primaries in the ++ / +- / -+ / -- corners; *zero* selected memberships change. The entire difference is in fitted shapes of retained galaxies. Ten primaries account for 55% and 68% of the sum of absolute object contributions to the primary and neighbour repeat differences, respectively (ranking separately). This localizes **noise in the attribution**, not the mean bias. [Paired numerical receipts](paired_diagnostics.json) include each scene, all cuts, and the exact per-role decomposition; [analysis script](paired_diagnostics.py).

## Why two optimizer seeds disagree

At **every central-cut selected detection** replay the original two fitter seeds on the saved coherent image or on the native mixed pixel stamp at its recorded centroid. Recover T (deconvolved second-moment size), g, and the fitter flag. All **4,030 selected fits** reproduce both original ellipticity components bit for bit and report flag zero. Initially 749 mixed corner pairs did not both replay exactly on a different CPU family; rerunning only the affected corners on the CPU family used for those images closed every mismatch. We used the stored original shapes throughout; no tolerance was relaxed. This recovered fit metadata, without new seeds, new detections, dropping IDs or changing the measurement.

Only **7.0%** of object–corner pairs switch T sign. Their median absolute g1 change is 0.426. For example, case 1120, primary 5667, on the unchanged -- pixels: T goes from −0.01086 to +0.04611 and g1 from −0.86730 to +0.86449. It passes FLUX_RADIUS > 3 pixels and MAG_AUTO < 25 both times. T is **not** the selection radius.

For an additive variance diagnostic let d_i be the weighted repeat difference for the specified response contrast of primary i, with d_ib its contribution from corners in branch b. The shares sum_i(d_i d_ib) / sum_i(d_i²) include covariance by construction and sum to one. The sign-switch pairs account for **94.4% / 46.7% / 67.2%** of this two-repeat squared variation in primary / neighbour / coherent R11. Both-positive pairs account for 53.1% for neighbours, so the neighbour scatter does not all come from T flips. These are two-seed diagnostics, not bias fractions or variance fractions over new scenes. The signed contributions above sum to the repeat difference exactly and require no variance approximation. See [fit trace and contributing primaries](fit_trace_report.json), [reduction](fit_trace_report.py), [original design](rule.md#estimator-and-limits), and [previous independent evidence for signed-size solutions](../constant_split_20261105/findings.md).

**Decision:** The pilot answers where the nine-cut model response moved. It cannot resolve percent-scale *absolute* physical component errors. Before buying more full-field extractions, the next precision test would average more predeclared random starts on these same images, using the original fit and selection and checking the remaining primary-sampling SE separately. Replacing the catalogue fit by a positivity cut or the best of several starts would change the quantity being calibrated. No change to the fitter, larger image campaign, model selection, or automated follow-up was made.

## What is measured

For the first time in this investigation, the two mixed corners change **one designated primary per full exposure**. Every other rendered source stays at the coherent shear of that exposure. The original noise, distant wings and detector context are retained. This replaces moving scenes onto a grid and avoids any assumption that cropping leaves detection unchanged. On all four corners we measure independent detection, matching, FLUX_RADIUS, MAG_AUTO and centred ngmix shape; a primary does not have to succeed on all four. See the [sampling and sign definitions](rule.md#sampling-and-four-corners) and [response conventions](../../CONVENTIONS.md#6-response-estimators).

The 2,048 IDs and weights are frozen. Each of the four original g1 scenes contributes 256 uniformly sampled objects in the union of coherent-leg matches and 256 from its complement. Selection is applied after sampling. The measured source is the GalSBI i-band population passed through the original r-labelled simulator column, with the original Sérsic profiles, radius conversion and PSF. The three GalSBI endpoints, C8 and emulator are fixed to the preceding [factor comparison](../neighbour_factor_swap_20261107/findings.md#final-conclusion-both-shear-axes).

## Independent controls

- Both existing A/B-split minus coherent pixel differences were reproduced in 32 target patches (eight per scene). The original 192-pixel surrounding-source search missed distant light in one first-case check. Without relaxing either tolerance, 29 final patches used 192 pixels and three required 384. Worst final 65-pixel-patch RMS difference was 0.0000208 image units; sky RMS was 0.616. Maximum pixel discrepancy was 0.000133. Native support was captured for all 2,048 individual primary light differences. These are independent checks against images not generated by the new intervention; they do not claim bitwise equality of mixed float32 exposures.
- Full-field coherent SExtractor catalogues replayed **byte for byte** on both signs in all four scenes. All 4,096 sampled primary/leg matches or nonmatches replayed the original assignments. We refit the same positions with two primary-specific optimizer seeds, paired across corners. The original entropy-seeded shapes are not the control for those fits.
- With roughly 500 selected targets per coherent leg, pooled target-cut measured R11 is **1.03394**. Delete-one-scene SE is 0.08262, conditional stratified primary-sampling SE is 0.13008, and the first-order two-seed estimate of optimizer Monte Carlo SE is 0.09603. These characterize different sources/conditioning and must not be summed in quadrature. They are not SEs on the as-yet unmeasured paired primary/neighbour model residuals. The two separately reduced optimizer repeats give 1.00949 and 1.05839. See [coherent-only summary](coherent_summary.json). Detection/photometry can be reused for future fit precision studies: original images, native primary deltas and exact centroids are retained.
- The model's first operational test found roundoff of 6.94e-18 when re-reducing unchanged shape numerators on a smaller batch. The calculation now reuses the original mass and shape numerator exactly and changes only the disk-transport term when neighbour shear flips. This does not change selection, models or tolerances.

## Execution history and interpretation

[Source and measurement receipts](pilot_receipts.json), [Slurm IDs](jobs.json), and [current status](status.json) link the measured images and frozen sample to the report. The fixed pilot comprises 4,096 mixed exposures plus the two archived coherent legs per field. Every extraction calls the native detector on the full noisy field. Full model inference retains the previous latent stream at the original primary block and compares the coherent response to the previous report for all three endpoints.

The final reducer reports primary, neighbour and coherent R11/R21, all nine cuts, predicted/actual usability and measured-selection interventions. It checks physical contrast additivity and estimates covariance using matched case deletions. It also reports stratified sampling uncertainty conditional on the four source fields, a within-case spatial-cluster sensitivity check, and the optimizer-repeat contribution. This g1 experiment cannot measure R22. The [completed results above](#completed-pilot-where-does-the-change-in-response-go) distinguish noisy absolute errors from better-determined paired model changes.

The 16-exposure operational run finished 0:0, 185.4–196.7 seconds per single-primary extraction (median 189.9 seconds). At its slowest measured rate, 4,096 exposures cost about 224 CPU-hours, within the declared cost gate. The full 2,048-primary variance pilot was released in four persistent, 32-CPU allocations; it reuses the 16 finished exposures. One GPU evaluates the three frozen flows. A dependent CPU report will reject incomplete, mismatched, or unpaired inputs. The owner cancelled the 07:29 CET follow-up before it fired; the recurring dispatcher is also off. No larger sample is running.

The first model scene (1000) passed its full-parent coherent response replay with maximum absolute difference 1.89e-15. Production mixed-exposure receipts are appearing. The fixed-budget precision projection uses finite-population corrections and stops each stratum at census; it does not extrapolate four-scene generalization or authorize additional draws.
